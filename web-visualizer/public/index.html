How the field resonates

Each quadrant is mapped to a sound. Pour atoms into one quadrant or across several to mix them. Wider piles get louder and brighter. Taller piles increase reverb and decay.

Navigation

Move

Drag with RMB to move about.

Zoom

Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom in and out.

Rotate

Drag with the middle mouse button to turn the view. Shift + RMB also turns it.

Emit and emitter size

Drag with LMB to emit with the largest emitter. Hold Shift while dragging to use the smallest emitter.

Move

Drag with one finger. The field slides under the emitter, which stays at the center of the screen.

Zoom

Pinch to zoom toward and away from the emitter.

Rotate

Twist two fingers to turn the field around the emitter.

Emit

Press the Emit button to pour atoms.

Emitter size

Drag the Emit button up or down to change the emitter size. You can adjust it while emitting.

Multitouch

Use these controls together. Emit while moving, rotating, or zooming the field, and select another material without stopping.

Move

The left stick or D-pad moves the emitter. At the edge of the view the plane scrolls.

Zoom

The right stick up and down zooms.

Rotate

The right stick left and right turns the view.

Emit and emitter size

Hold the right trigger to emit atoms. A light pull makes a single stream; pulling farther widens the emitter. The left trigger always makes a single stream. You can keep emitting while moving, rotating, or zooming.

Materials

You can choose different materials for the atoms you emit. Each material interacts with the others to change the shape of piles of atoms.

Block

A block sinks beneath whatever is underneath it. A pile of blocks will lose height as it sinks.

Sand

Piles into gaps and has a tendency to spread out.

Diffuse

Spreads and floats away.

Erode

Cuts through a pile.
